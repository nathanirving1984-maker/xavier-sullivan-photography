About

From the ballpark
to the front porch.

I've spent the last five years behind the lens in professional and collegiate sports, most recently with the San Francisco Giants, and before that with the Reno Aces and Nevada Athletics. Sports photography is unforgiving: the light changes, the moment passes, and there are no second takes.

That discipline translates directly to real estate. I plan around the sun, compose every frame with intent, and deliver images that make a buyer stop scrolling. Every listing gets the same care I'd give a walk-off home run.
